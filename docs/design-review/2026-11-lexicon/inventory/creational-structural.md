## Top 3 by payoff against cost

1. **One shared outbound-HTTP module.** Promote discovery's `providerhttp` (GetJSON/GetBytesCapped, limiters, StatusError) and the egress transport into `internal/shared`, and route acquisition (catalogpin, chromaprint, deezerpreview, ytmsearch), observe notifiers and the InnerTube budget through it.
   Payoff: about 10 hand-rolled request/cap/status/pace copies collapse into one decorator stack, and InnerTube stops bypassing the dial guard and retry. Cost: medium (one package move, about 6 adapters).
2. **A facade for acquisition and catalog, and doors sized for callers.** Give each a `New(Deps)` like discovery and move the 52 constructor calls out of `catalog_wiring.go`. In-feature tests test their own package rather than importing the root door, so the 132 + 120 test-only aliases can go.
   Payoff: the largest cut in door width and app coupling. Cost: high, but each feature can be done on its own.
3. **Cmd tools reuse the app's factories.** Replace the 10 hand-copied `openPool` functions with `database.NewPool(ctx, url, 2)`, and export one downloader / YTM-search constructor that both app and `journey` use (journey has already drifted).
   Payoff: removes live drift. Cost: low (mechanical, scripts only).
