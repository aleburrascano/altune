## Top 3 by payoff against cost

1. **One home for Track's invariants.** Make Track's fields unexported and route every write through its methods, including acquisition's SQL writers and the relabel path. That closes the relabel path that skips the year check and the fake copy of the transitions, and the shared-table design gets its full list of writers. Cost: medium, because the shared-table design is already planned.
2. **Value objects for repeated rules.** Move `LengthTolerance` to the kernel (and delete discovery's copy), add `Seconds`/`Year`/`TrackNumber` types, and build the SQL grade order from `grade.worstToBest`. Each is a small change, and each removes a copy that drifts on its own. Cost: low.
3. **Move acquisition's two bridges to ga/app and turn depguard on for old code.** That gives ACLs one home and makes the feature boundary checked by tooling, not by memory. Cost: low, since it moves 6 files and adds no new logic.
