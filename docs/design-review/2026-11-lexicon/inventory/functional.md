## Top 3 by payoff against cost

1. **Turn core purity into a lint rule, then move the shell out of core.** Today 30 of 70 Go core files and 19 of 85 client core files do I/O. A depguard rule and a `no-restricted-imports` rule cost about an hour and keep core pure from then on. Moving the files (start with the 10 `features/detail/core/use*.ts` hooks, `handledNow` and `idfloor`) can go one package at a time.
2. **One `Result<T,E>` in client shared/failure, and NotFound from Go adapters.** The client has three ok-union shapes, four throw-then-catch-null sites and six status classifiers, which become one type plus one place that maps status to a kind. Go loses 10 hand-written nil-to-NotFound conversions and a nil-dereference risk, with a small change in track_repo.go.
3. **Enable Go's `exhaustive` linter, and enable React Compiler with a measurement.** The linter makes 18 catch-all enum switches explicit, at the cost of a config line plus the fixes. The compiler replaces 78 unmeasured hand memos with one mechanism. Measure renders before and after, because the gain is unverified today.
