## Top 3 by payoff against cost

1. **Turn on `-race` for go-api.** Add one flag at `pr-checks.yml:81` and `scripts/precheck.sh:150`. Overseer already runs it.
   go-api has 102 parallel tests, concurrent schedulers and 103 sleeps, and today nothing detects a data race among them. Expect the first run to find real races.
2. **Contract suites for the stateful fakes that lack one.** Start with `acquisitiontest.Ledger` (planned). Then merge the two copied playback `inMemoryQueueRepo`s into one memory adapter with a suite. Then cover the discovery stores (identity, favorites, history, events, vocabulary, unreleased).
   The catalog `contract/` package is the template. About 9 ports today are tested against fakes nobody has checked against Postgres.
3. **One go-api clock and timer port with a manual fake in sharedtest.** It replaces 15 hand-written fake clocks, removes the sleeps from `scheduler_test.go`, and lets the 11 decision sites take `now`.
   It fixes the biggest flake source and the house rule against wall-clock time (q/maintainability.md:49) together. The cost is moderate, because constructors change at the composition root.
