## Top 3 by payoff against cost

1. **One Tracker, built in `ga/app`, bounded and waited.** Pass a single Tracker into search, ownership and discography. Launch takes a label, a budget and a max-in-flight with a drop counter. Shutdown waits on it once, before `CloseDatastores`.
   This closes the two un-waited trackers, the unbounded per-search spawning and the 5 copied timeouts. It is a small diff in `tracker.go`, `app_shutdown.go` and three constructors.
2. **Deadlines on every scheduler queue write, through `runloop.Detach(ctx, budget)`.** Replace the 7 `context.Background()` calls in `scheduler.go:448-482` and `scheduler_heartbeat.go:31`, then fold the 12 WithoutCancel sites into the helper.
   A hung Postgres connection can no longer pin an acquisition worker forever or hide lease expiry. The change is mechanical and has no API impact.
3. **`fanout.Group` recovers panics and becomes the only bounded fan-out.** A 3-line guard in `fanout.go:15` fixes the crash path in `catalog_titles.go:85` and `unreleased/service.go:173`. Then move the 8 hand-rolled WaitGroup sites and the 2 ytdlp errgroups onto it, and add `runloop.WaitWithin` for the 6 copied wait-with-deadline blocks.
   The guard lands first and on its own. The migration removes the per-site drift in recover, logging and limits that this topic and errors.md both found.
