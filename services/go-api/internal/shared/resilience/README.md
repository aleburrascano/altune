Infrastructure: `resilience` owns how outbound calls wait, retry and stop: the backoff between attempts, the circuit breaker, the mark that says a request may be sent again, and the error for a request refused for waiting too long.

- `BackoffPolicy` is how long to wait before the next attempt of anything that retries: exponential from a base, bounded, with a jitter shape the caller names. It is built only by `MustCappedBackoff(base, cap, opts...)` or `MustUncappedBackoff(base, opts...)`, which panic at startup on a base or cap that is not positive, a cap below the base, a factor below 2 or a spread outside [0, 1]. `WithBackoffFactor(n)` sets the growth per attempt (2 when unset). `WithBackoffSpread(f)` picks symmetric jitter. `Ceiling(attempt)` is the wait without jitter; `DelayAt(attempt, unit)` places the wait by `unit` in [0, 1), so a test passes the point it wants; `Delay(attempt)` draws `unit` at random.
- `CircuitBreaker` is the circuit-breaker protocol that search's provider calls and playback's now-playing enrichment share. One breaker guards one dependency. `Allow(ctx)` returns a `CircuitAdmission` and whether the call may go. Settle every admitted call exactly once: `Success`, `Failure`, or `Release` when the outcome says nothing about the dependency's health. The circuit opens when `Threshold` failures accumulate, or when a probe fails. It stays open for `OpenFor` after the last failure, then admits one probe. A probe holds the slot until it settles or `ProbeLease` passes. Only a probe's success closes it. `CircuitBreakerConfig.OnChange` is called under the breaker's lock for each transition (`CircuitTripped`, `CircuitProbing`, `ProbeLeaseExpired`, `CircuitRecovered`), so it must be quick and must not call back into the breaker. A caller that guards several dependencies keeps one `CircuitBreaker` per key. A `Threshold` below 1 counts as 1; a nil `Now` is the wall clock. `OpenFor` and `ProbeLease` must be positive: a zero `OpenFor` admits a probe at once, and a zero `ProbeLease` lets every caller probe. A success from a call admitted before the circuit tripped neither closes it nor resets the failure count of a later run, and a probe that lost its lease cannot free the slot of the probe that replaced it.
- `MarkRetrySafe(ctx)` and `IsRetrySafe(ctx)` are the one mark a caller puts on a request context to say that sending it again is harmless, so the outbound retry transport may repeat it. A POST that only reads, or that the upstream dedupes, is marked; an unmarked POST is sent once. `platform/outbound` sets the mark on the POSTs it builds and its retry transport reads it, and a download test observes it, which is why it lives in `shared`.
- `ErrQueueTimeout` and `MaxQueueWait` are the one error that says an outbound request was refused because it would have waited too long for its provider's rate limit. `platform/outbound` returns an error that wraps `ErrQueueTimeout` and `context.DeadlineExceeded`. `search` aliases it as `ErrProviderRateLimitQueueTimeout`, so its circuit breaker, fan-out and metrics treat the refusal as a shed request and never as a provider failure.
